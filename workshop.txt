description=Shout Shockwave turns your shout into a weapon. Train the new [b]Shout[/b] skill and a yell staggers, knocks down, dazes and finally hurts the zombies around you, and shatters the windows of nearby buildings.
description=
description=[b]Each Shout level unlocks a stronger effect, and the louder you are, the further it reaches.[/b]
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Skill unlocks:[/b] level 2 stagger, level 4 knock down, level 6 daze (zombies lose their target for a few seconds), level 8 damage. Levels 0-1 have no effect yet. Each new effect hits the closest zombies, and the earlier ones reach further. A zombie hit by a stronger effect also gets all the weaker ones.
description=[*][b]Louder shout, bigger blast:[/b] Strength makes you a little louder, Shout a lot louder; at low levels you are a bit quieter than vanilla. A Megaphone in your main hand, or a Whistle or Harmonica in hand, raises it further, as in vanilla. A shout while sneaking is much weaker.
description=[*][b]Distance:[/b] the blast weakens with distance. Tough zombies resist, wounded ones go down more easily. Distance is measured the way vanilla measures sound: zombies in another room, or on the other side of the line between indoors and outdoors, count as a bit further away, and the floors above and below are reached too.
description=[*][b]Windows:[/b] from Shout level 8 the blast breaks nearby building windows, with the glass sound and house alarms. Boarded and open windows are spared, and vehicle windows are never touched.
description=[*][b]Endurance, no cooldown:[/b] every shout costs endurance (less with higher Fitness and Shout). The more tired you are, the quieter your voice and the weaker the blast; with no endurance left you shout at half volume and the usual vanilla exhaustion penalties apply. On multiplayer servers the cost now sticks (fixed in 0.1.3: endurance used to refill within a second, so shouts could be spammed).
description=[*][b]XP:[/b] shouting alone trains Shout up to level 2. After that, XP comes from zombies your shout affects (capped per shout). Knocking zombies down also gives a little Strength XP.
description=[*][b]Effects:[/b] a shockwave ring on the ground that grows with your level, a low rumble (level 4+) and dust on knocked-down zombies (level 8+).
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Limits[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]A shout from inside a vehicle is a plain vanilla shout, with no effects.
description=[*]When your shout is quieter than vanilla (low skill or tired), tamed animals do not come to it.
description=[*]Tested in single player. Multiplayer is not tested yet.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Lua only, no vanilla files replaced. Shouts from the Q key, the emote wheel and the controller all go through it.
description=[*]In multiplayer the server works out the effects and the client that runs each zombie applies them, so the mod must be on the server and on the clients.
description=[*]Safe to add to an existing save. Removing it removes the Shout skill.
description=[/list]
