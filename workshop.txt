title=[42.22.2] Shout Shockwave
description=[h1]Shout Shockwave[/h1]
description=Your shout becomes a weapon. Train the new [b]Shout[/b] skill and a yell staggers, knocks down, dazes and finally hurts the zombies around you, and shatters the windows of nearby buildings.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Shout skill unlocks the effects.[/b] Level 0-1: just a louder voice. Level 2: stagger. Level 4: knock down. Level 6: daze (zombies lose their target for a few seconds). Level 8: damage. Each new effect hits closest zombies; the earlier ones reach further. A zombie hit by a stronger effect also gets all the weaker ones.
description=[*][b]Louder shout, bigger blast.[/b] Strength makes you a little louder, Shout a lot louder. A megaphone in hand, a whistle or a harmonica raise it further, as in vanilla. The strength of the blast falls with distance; tough zombies resist, wounded ones go down easier.
description=[*][b]Windows.[/b] From Shout level 8 the blast breaks building windows nearby (glass sound and house alarms included). Boarded and open windows are spared, and vehicle windows are never touched.
description=[*][b]Shouting tires you.[/b] Every shout costs endurance (less with Fitness). Tired, your voice gets quieter and the blast weaker. No cooldowns.
description=[*][b]XP.[/b] Shouting alone trains Shout up to level 2. After that, XP comes from zombies your shout affects (capped per shout). Knocked-down zombies also give a little Strength XP.
description=[*][b]Effects.[/b] A shockwave ring on the ground that grows with your level, dust on knocked-down zombies (level 8+), and a low rumble (level 4+). Ring and dust can be turned off in Mod Options.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=Turn stagger, knockdown, daze, damage and window breaking on or off; damage multiplier; endurance cost multiplier; window range multiplier.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Lua only, no vanilla files replaced. Shouts from the Q key, the emote wheel, vehicle passengers and the controller all go through it. On servers the server works out the effects and the client that runs each zombie applies them, so the mod must be on both server and clients. Safe to add to an existing save; removing it removes the Shout skill. Multiplayer not tested yet.
description=
description=[h2]한국어[/h2]
description=외치기(Q)를 무기로 만듭니다. 새 스킬 [b]외침[/b]을 키우면 고함이 주변 좀비를 비틀거리게(2레벨), 넘어뜨리고(4레벨), 멍하게 하고(6레벨), 피해를 주며(8레벨), 8레벨부터는 가까운 건물 유리창을 깹니다(차량 유리는 안 깨짐). 소리가 클수록(근력·외침 레벨, 메가폰·호루라기) 멀리까지 닿고, 거리가 멀수록·좀비가 튼튼할수록 약해집니다. 외칠 때마다 지구력이 들고, 지치면 목소리가 작아집니다. 외치기만으로는 2레벨까지 오르고, 그 뒤로는 좀비에게 효과를 내야 오릅니다. 효과마다 샌드박스 옵션으로 끄고 켤 수 있고, 링·먼지 연출은 모드 옵션에서 끌 수 있습니다. 멀티는 아직 확인하지 않았습니다.
